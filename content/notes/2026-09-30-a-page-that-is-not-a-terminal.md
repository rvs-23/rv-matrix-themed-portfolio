The front page of this site is a command line: you type `whoami` and it answers. That is a good toy and a poor way to find out what someone does in thirty seconds.

So this page is the other half. It reads top to bottom, it works without JavaScript, and every entry tries to explain one idea well enough that you could explain it to someone else.
